# Features

## Core Features

### 1. Course & Assignment Management

- Create and manage academic courses
- Add assignments, quizzes, labs, and exams
- Store due dates, priorities, estimated effort, and course weights

---

### 2. Dynamic Schedule Generation

- Automatically distribute tasks across available study time
- Balance workload across days and weeks
- Prevent excessive daily workload concentration

---

### 3. Adaptive Rescheduling

- Recalculate schedules when tasks are missed
- Redistribute unfinished work automatically
- Adjust future schedules based on updated workload conditions

---

### 4. Burnout & Overload Detection

- Detect high-workload periods
- Warn users about unrealistic schedules
- Identify sustained overload trends

---

### 5. Syllabus Parsing

- Upload course syllabi as PDFs
- Extract assignment deadlines and exam dates
- Automatically generate academic timelines

---

### 6. Productivity Analytics

- Visualize workload trends
- Display study distribution across courses
- Generate workload heatmaps and scheduling insights

---
